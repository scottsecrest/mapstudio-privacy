MapStudio Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

MapStudio is provided by Scott Secrest. For privacy questions or access/deletion requests, email scott.secrest@gmail.com.

Simulated tours and optional location

Tour position and speed are simulated. You can enter places, calculate routes and play tours without location permission. Only Locate Me or Follow My Location requests iOS When In Use access. The app uses current location and GPS speed in memory to center/follow the map and display device speed. It does not record a GPS travel history or track in the background. Stop following in Tour options or revoke access in iPhone Settings. Denying permission leaves manually entered tours usable.

Apple map services

MapStudio uses Apple MapKit for maps, place searches, directions, nearby places and Look Around. Search text, selected route points and coordinates needed for requested content are sent to Apple. Viewing your actual location can request map content for that area. Apple processes its services under its own terms: Apple Maps & Privacy and Apple Privacy Policy. Imagery is available provider content, not live footage.

Local storage and files you share

The app stores interface preferences, idle-orbit choices, optional camera defaults (which can include a focus coordinate), and the last tour configuration in app preferences. That configuration can contain chosen places, coordinates, notes and settings. Route geometry and playback position are not restored after termination; routes must be calculated again. App data is subject to your iOS backup settings.

Tour files contain the places, coordinates, notes and settings you choose. Saving uses your selected Files provider, which may sync or back up copies. Sharing creates a temporary tour file and opens the system share sheet for the recipients/services you choose. Deleting the app removes its local preferences and temporary files; delete independently saved or shared copies through their storage provider. Backed-up and recipient copies are managed separately.

Beta diagnostics and feedback

MapStudio includes no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK, and no developer-operated backend or automatic diagnostic upload service. It has no app account. This does not mean no data is received during testing.

Apple TestFlight automatically collects usage and crash information and shares it with the developer. It can include invitation identity, device/OS details, installs, sessions and crash logs. Feedback you submit can include comments, screenshots and diagnostics. Apple says it keeps beta feedback for one year and may keep crash/usage information until bugs are resolved; other installation records support Apple's service purposes. See TestFlight & Privacy.

From the effective date above, we use beta feedback and diagnostics to investigate bugs and improve MapStudio. We avoid separately exporting or storing tester information unless a specific issue needs it. For copies we control, including support email and issue attachments, we delete them when no longer needed for that investigation. We do not share TestFlight tester information with third parties. Review screenshots, attachments and tour files for personal information before sending them.

Privacy requests and Apple's records

Contact the email above about information we control; we use your request details to respond. We cannot promise deletion from Apple's systems or change Apple's retention rules. Apple's Data and Privacy page provides ways to view/manage data held by Apple. We will explain the scope and limits of any action on your request.

Updates

We will update this policy and its effective date when our app behavior or handling practices change.
